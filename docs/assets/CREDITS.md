# Image credits

The before and after pictures in the README were made from public-domain images. Each was
set as the wallpaper of a test GNOME Shell, captured with `vividctl` off and on (value 400), and the
two captures were joined in the middle (`scripts/split-screenshot.py`, see
`extensions/gnome/test/screenshots.sh`).

| File | Wallpaper | Credit | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| `before-after-cosmic-cliffs.jpg` | "Cosmic Cliffs" in the Carina Nebula, James Webb Space Telescope | NASA, ESA, CSA, STScI | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NASA%E2%80%99s_Webb_Reveals_Cosmic_Cliffs,_Glittering_Landscape_of_Star_Birth.jpg) |
| `before-after-mystic-mountain.jpg` | "Mystic Mountain" (a landscape in the Carina Nebula), Hubble Space Telescope | NASA, ESA, Mario Livio (STScI) and the Hubble 20th Anniversary Team (STScI) | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hubble_Captures_Spectacular_%22Landscape%22_in_the_Carina_Nebula_(2010-13-2718).tiff) |
| `before-after-pillars-of-creation.jpg` | The Pillars of Creation in the Eagle Nebula, Hubble Space Telescope, 2014 visible-light image (WFC3/UVIS, denoised), cropped to the three pillar tops | NASA, ESA and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA) | [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pillars_of_creation_2014_HST_WFC3-UVIS_full-res_denoised.jpg) |
